1. Define Problem / Problem Understanding
Domain Strategy

The global automotive sector is undergoing a generational shift from internal combustion engines (ICE) toward electric powertrains, accelerated by decarbonization commitments, battery cost parity, and government mandates. Organizations across the value chain face challenges in synthesizing high-volume technical, financial, and geographical market data.

Three Dedicated Stakeholder Personas Addressed:
  • Emily Johnson (EV Market Strategy Director): Needs executive-level insights on category revenue contribution, battery technology adoption curves, driving range vs customer satisfaction thresholds, and high-growth vehicle segments.
  • David Chen (Automotive BI Manager): Monitors manufacturer market share trajectories, battery chemistry distribution, geographic manufacturing vs export corridors, and pricing power.
  • Dr. Sarah Williams (Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst): Investigates battery innovations (LFP, NMC, NCA, Sodium-Ion, Solid-State), lifecycle durability (cycles), energy consumption (kWh/100km), fast-charging turnaround, and total carbon abatement.
2. Data Collection & Extraction from Database
Data Engineering

A statistically robust, industry-accurate dataset of 6,803 records spanning 2019 to 2025 was engineered covering 12 global automakers (Tesla, BYD, Volkswagen, Hyundai-Kia, BMW, Rivian, NIO, Volvo, Tata Motors, Proterra, Ola Electric, SAIC/MG) across 6 vehicle categories and 5 battery chemistries.

Database Storage & Schema:
SQLite 3 database (ev_market_analytics.db) with dual-architecture: Normalized Star-Schema (fact_ev_records connected to 4 dimension tables) and Denormalized Analytics table (ev_market_analytics) with B-Tree composite indexes.
SQL Analytical Operations:
12+ production-grade queries utilizing Common Table Expressions (CTEs), Window Functions (RANK(), DENSE_RANK(), LAG(), SUM() OVER()), grouping sets, and analytical views tailored for each persona.
Tableau Connection Protocol:
Connected to Tableau via SQLite3 ODBC driver and direct live-extract CSV synchronization, enabling automated data refreshes.
3. Data Preparation & Feature Engineering
Feature Store

The data preparation pipeline (analysis_and_prep/data_preparation.py) cleanses raw records, validates ranges, handles categorical standardizations, and engineers critical analytical features:

  • Price_per_Range_km: Evaluates price efficiency per unit distance.
  • Charge_Speed_km_per_min: Measures rapid-charging turnaround capability.
  • TCO_5_Year_USD: 5-year Total Cost of Ownership factoring electricity tariffs ($0.16/kWh) and maintenance.
  • Carbon_Abatement_kg_per_USD: Quantifies environmental return on investment per capital expenditure.
  • Market_Competitiveness_Score: Multi-attribute index weighting range, reliability, and price.
