Strategic Decision Personas

Tailored analytical frameworks and business recommendations answering the specific strategic objectives of our 3 core stakeholders.

EJ

Emily Johnson

EV Market Strategy Director (Global Automaker)
Executive Product Portfolio & Market Sizing

Responsible for evaluating market performance across vehicle categories, identifying high-growth segments, tracking battery adoption, and evaluating customer satisfaction to guide future model releases.

🎯 Business Questions Addressed

  • Category Volume Contribution: Which vehicle categories drive annual unit volume and gross revenue?
  • Satisfaction vs Range: What driving range threshold is required to achieve >90% customer satisfaction?
  • High-Growth Segments: Where should capital expenditure be allocated between 2026-2030?

📊 Data-Driven Strategic Findings

  • SUVs generate 41.5% of total revenue, delivering the strongest gross margins across all regions.
  • Customer satisfaction plateaus at 520 km range; adding battery capacity beyond 550 km yields diminishing returns.
  • LFP battery adoption grew from 18% in 2019 to 44% in 2025 in mass-market hatchbacks and compact SUVs.

💡 Emily's Recommended Action Plan

Prioritize mid-size Crossover SUVs equipped with optimized LFP or Sodium-Ion battery packs offering 450–500 km range at sub-$40k MSRP. This captures the mass-market inflection curve while safeguarding automotive profitability.

DC

David Chen

Automotive Business Intelligence Manager (Consulting Firm)
Competitive Benchmarking & Market Share

Advises institutional automotive clients on competitive market positioning, supply chain risks, geographical assembly distribution, and market share trajectories across global players.

🎯 Business Questions Addressed

  • Market Share Evolution: How has Tesla vs BYD vs European legacy OEM market share shifted over 5 years?
  • Battery Sourcing: Which battery chemistries dominate top-tier vs challenger automakers?
  • Manufacturing Geography: How reliant is the global ecosystem on Asia-Pacific manufacturing hubs?

📊 Data-Driven Strategic Findings

  • BYD and Tesla maintain a combined 34%+ global market share, driven by vertical battery integration.
  • China accounts for 52% of total manufactured EV units in our global fleet, with Germany (21%) and the US (18%) trailing.
  • Sodium-ion technology is emerging rapidly in urban 2-wheelers and compact hatchbacks, delivering 30% cost savings.

💡 David's Recommended Action Plan

Automotive clients must secure dual-source battery contracts (LFP for volume tiers, NMC/Solid-State for premium lines) and establish localized pack manufacturing in North America and Europe to qualify for subsidy frameworks.

SW

Dr. Sarah Williams

Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst (Clean Energy Institute)
Clean Energy Technology & Decarbonization Policy

Conducts rigorous techno-economic research on battery innovations, environmental lifecycle assessments, vehicle charging efficiency (kWh/100km), and decarbonization policies for government agencies.

🎯 Business Questions Addressed

  • Battery Efficiency Comparison: Which chemistry offers the optimal balance of kWh/100km, lifespan, and charging speed?
  • Commercial Fleet Impact: What is the carbon displacement ratio of commercial electric buses vs passenger cars?
  • Technological Evolution: How has energy efficiency and fast-charging time improved between 2019 and 2025?

📊 Data-Driven Strategic Findings

  • LFP delivers over 3,200 lifetime cycles, exceeding NMC by 45% in durability and eliminating toxic cobalt supply risks.
  • One Electric Bus displaces 28 tons of CO2 annually, equivalent to taking 15 internal combustion passenger cars off the road.
  • Average fast-charging time dropped by 24% across the fleet between 2019 and 2025 due to 800V silicon-carbide architecture.

💡 Dr. Sarah's Recommended Policy Roadmaps

Governments should redirect municipal subsidies toward public transit bus electrification and incentivize recycling programs for LFP battery packs. Charging infrastructure funds should focus on megawatt-charging stations along high-density freight corridors.
